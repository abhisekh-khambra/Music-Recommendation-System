## Notes

- These recordings are synthetic (text-to-speech). They check that the audio pipeline and this evaluation work; they are NOT evidence of how the voice-emotion model or voiceprints perform on real human speech. Record real conversations (with consent) for that — see evaluation/audio/README.md.
- Voice-only accuracy compares the voice model's 4 classes (neutral/happy/angry/sad) with the gold emotion collapsed onto them; responses whose gold emotion has no voice class (NOSTALGIA, STRESS, ANXIETY, LOVE) are excluded — 15 of 27 responses were scorable.
- Speaker separation is scored on the labelled turn boundaries, under the better cluster-to-role mapping.
